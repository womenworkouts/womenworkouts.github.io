How it works

Answer a few questions, get your plan and finish your first workout before you close the app.

  1. Tell us about you. Age, height, weight, current fitness level and how many days a week you can realistically train.
  2. Pick your goal and focus area. Lose weight, build muscle or keep fit — then choose the areas you want the plan weighted toward.
  3. Start your first workout. Guided exercise by exercise, with a rest timer between sets.
  4. Log and adjust. Record weight and measurements as you go. The plan and your daily calorie target move with you.

Frequently asked questions

Still stuck? The support page has more, or email us directly.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every workout is designed for home and needs nothing but floor space. A mat makes floor work more comfortable, but it is not required.

Is Women Workouts free?

The app is free to download and includes free workouts. An optional subscription unlocks the full plan and premium features. You can use the free part without ever subscribing — see the subscription terms for details.

Is it suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. During setup you choose your fitness level. Beginners start at a gentle pace with shorter sessions, and the plan builds up as you progress.

Where is my workout and body data stored?

Your workout history, weight entries and body measurements are stored in the app's database on your own device. If you create an account, only your account details are held by Firebase Authentication. The full picture is in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete account. You can also email us from your registered address. Step-by-step instructions are on the account deletion page.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Danish, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
